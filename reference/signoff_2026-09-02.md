## Definitions

- **Population:** new Type A/B demand arising FY2022–FY2026 — every person
  whose first Type A/B approval (or first Type A/B admission, if never
  approved) fell between 1 April 2021 and 31 March 2026, and who was not
  already in residential care at that event.
- **Residency at the demand event:** Provincial Registry latest mapped address
  in the three fiscal years before the event, else the Strata `address_h`
  version effective on the demand date, mapped through the same Statistics
  Canada postal geography. Town of Cochrane is the census subdivision.
- **Outcome:** first Type A/B admission at or after the demand event, in the
  Calgary and Edmonton Strata placement instances, on or before 31 March 2026.
- **B** = any non-Town resident placed in Cochrane, including Cochrane-catchment
  residents (`b_catchment` = 6 kept visible).
- **D** = *no Type A/B placement observed in the Calgary/Edmonton Strata
  placement source by 31 March 2026.* **It is not provincial unmet demand.**

## Caveats that travel with every figure

1. **Source coverage.** Placements are observed in the Calgary and Edmonton
   Strata instances only (936 sites, 294,659 admissions; 7 sites and 204
   admissions in all other zones). A Town resident placed in Central, South
   or North zone appears in D. Applies to all of D.
2. **D is conditioned on the source**, not on need.
3. **Wait-time analysis** must state which approval field it uses.
4. **B = non-Town.** Revisit only if the consultant redefines "Cochrane
   resident" from Town to catchment.
5. **The residency method is the registry**, not the placement record; the
   reasons are in the README and remain the methodological core.
